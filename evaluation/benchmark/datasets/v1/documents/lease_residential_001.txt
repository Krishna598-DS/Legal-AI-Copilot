RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made as of June 1, 2026, between Ridgeview Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Priya Nair ("Tenant") for the property located at 48 Alder Street, Unit 3B, Portland, OR ("Premises").

Clause 1 - Term
This Lease begins on June 1, 2026, and continues on a month-to-month basis thereafter until terminated as described in Clause 6.

Clause 2 - Rent
Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,200, due in full on the 1st day of each calendar month. Rent paid after the 5th day of the month is considered late.

Clause 3 - Late Fee
If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $75, in addition to the rent owed.

Clause 4 - Security Deposit
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,200 prior to occupancy. The deposit will be held to cover damages beyond normal wear and tear and will be returned within 30 days of move-out, less any lawful deductions.

Clause 5 - Maintenance and Repairs
Landlord is responsible for maintaining the Premises in habitable condition, including major structural and system repairs. Tenant is responsible for routine maintenance and any repair costs under $150 arising from Tenant's use of the Premises.

Clause 6 - Termination and Notice
Either Party may terminate this month-to-month tenancy by providing sixty (60) days written notice to the other Party.

Clause 7 - Subletting
Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the prior written consent of Landlord.

Clause 8 - Utilities
Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, and internet service. Landlord is responsible for water, sewer, and trash collection.

Clause 9 - Pets
No pets are permitted on the Premises without Landlord's prior written approval and, if approved, a separate pet addendum and deposit.

Clause 10 - Governing Law
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
