MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026, between Northbridge Analytics, Inc. ("Northbridge") and Fenwick Data Partners LLC ("Fenwick"), each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

Clause 1 - Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship and, in connection with that discussion, may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to each other.

Clause 2 - Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any technical, financial, or business information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether in written, oral, or electronic form, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

Clause 3 - Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure, as shown by written records; (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction.

Clause 4 - Obligations of Receiving Party
The receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely to evaluate the potential business relationship and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

Clause 5 - Duration of Confidentiality
The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement shall survive for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure of the applicable Confidential Information, regardless of when this Agreement is terminated.

Clause 6 - Required Disclosure
If the receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, provide the disclosing Party with prompt written notice before making such disclosure.

Clause 7 - No License
Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license or right to the other Party's Confidential Information beyond the limited purpose described in Clause 1.

Clause 8 - Termination
This Agreement may be terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party. Termination of this Agreement does not affect the surviving confidentiality obligations described in Clause 5.

Clause 9 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

Clause 10 - Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements, written or oral, relating to the same subject matter.
