EQUIPMENT PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Equipment Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 5, 2026, between Cascade Manufacturing Inc. ("Seller") and Bluepeak Foods LLC ("Buyer") for the sale of one industrial packaging machine, Model PX-400 ("Equipment").

Clause 1 - Purchase Price
The total purchase price for the Equipment is $85,000, payable as a $25,000 deposit upon signing and $60,000 upon delivery.

Clause 2 - Delivery
Seller shall deliver the Equipment within forty-five (45) days of the effective date of this Agreement to Buyer's facility in Salem, Oregon.

Clause 3 - Inspection Period
Buyer shall have ten (10) business days after delivery to inspect the Equipment and reject any non-conforming units.

Clause 4 - Warranty
Seller warrants the Equipment against defects in material and workmanship for twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

Clause 5 - Title and Risk of Loss
Title to the Equipment passes to Buyer upon receipt of full payment. Risk of loss passes to Buyer upon delivery.

Clause 6 - Taxes
Buyer is responsible for all applicable sales and use taxes arising from this purchase.

Clause 7 - Limitation of Liability
Seller's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
