INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 10, 2026, between Orbit Design Studio ("Client") and Jamie Ruiz ("Contractor") for UI/UX design services.

Clause 1 - Services
Contractor shall provide UI/UX design services as described in Exhibit A.

Clause 2 - Compensation
Client shall pay Contractor $75 per hour, invoiced monthly, payable within fifteen (15) days of invoice receipt.

Clause 3 - Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee of Client. Client shall not withhold taxes, and Contractor is solely responsible for Contractor's own taxes and benefits.

Clause 4 - Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon fourteen (14) days' written notice. Client may terminate immediately for Contractor's material breach.

Clause 5 - Ownership of Work Product
All deliverables produced under this Agreement are works made for hire and shall be owned by Client upon receipt of full payment.

Clause 6 - Confidentiality
Contractor shall keep Client's confidential information secret during the engagement and for three (3) years afterward.

Clause 7 - No Non-Compete
This Agreement does not restrict Contractor from performing design services for other clients, including businesses that compete with Client.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington.
