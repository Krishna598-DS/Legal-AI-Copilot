EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, between Solara Robotics, Inc. ("Company") and Marcus Webb ("Employee").

Clause 1 - Position
Employee is employed as Senior Firmware Engineer, reporting to the Director of Engineering, and shall perform the duties customarily associated with that role.

Clause 2 - Compensation
Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary of $95,000, payable in accordance with Company's standard payroll practices.

Clause 3 - Benefits
Employee is eligible to participate in Company's group health insurance plan and shall accrue fifteen (15) days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to Company-observed holidays.

Clause 4 - At-Will Employment and Termination Notice
Employment under this Agreement is at-will. Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party.

Clause 5 - Confidentiality
Employee agrees not to disclose Company's trade secrets or proprietary information, both during employment and after termination of this Agreement.

Clause 6 - Non-Competition
For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of employment, Employee shall not, within a fifty (50) mile radius of Company's headquarters, work for or consult with any business that directly competes with Company's robotics products.

Clause 7 - Intellectual Property
All inventions, designs, and works created by Employee within the scope of employment shall be the sole property of Company.

Clause 8 - Expense Reimbursement
Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable, pre-approved business expenses incurred in the performance of Employee's duties, submitted with appropriate documentation.

Clause 9 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
