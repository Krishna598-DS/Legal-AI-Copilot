GENERAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This General Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 2, 2026, between Aiden Cole and Priya Shah (together, the "Partners"), forming "Cole & Shah Architecture Partners" (the "Partnership").

Clause 1 - Formation
The Partners form a general partnership under the laws of the State of Colorado.

Clause 2 - Capital Contributions
Cole shall contribute $60,000 in capital. Shah shall contribute $40,000 in capital.

Clause 3 - Profit and Loss Sharing
Profits and losses shall be shared 60% to Cole and 40% to Shah, proportional to their capital contributions.

Clause 4 - Management
The Partners share equal management authority. Decisions involving expenditures over $10,000 require unanimous consent of both Partners.

Clause 5 - Withdrawal of a Partner
A Partner may withdraw from the Partnership upon ninety (90) days' written notice. The remaining Partner shall have the right of first refusal to purchase the withdrawing Partner's interest at fair market value.

Clause 6 - Dissolution
The Partnership shall dissolve upon unanimous written agreement of the Partners, the death of a Partner, or as otherwise required by law.

Clause 7 - Liability
The Partners are jointly and severally liable for the obligations of the Partnership, as is standard for general partnerships.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
