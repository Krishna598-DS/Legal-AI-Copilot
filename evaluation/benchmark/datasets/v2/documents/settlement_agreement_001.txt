SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

This Settlement Agreement and Release ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 20, 2026, between Harbor Freight Movers LLC ("Company") and Tomas Alvarez ("Claimant"), resolving a wage dispute claim.

Clause 1 - Recitals
Claimant alleged unpaid overtime wages against Company. The Parties wish to resolve this dispute without any admission of liability.

Clause 2 - Settlement Payment
Company shall pay Claimant $18,500, less applicable withholdings, within fourteen (14) days of the Effective Date.

Clause 3 - No Admission of Liability
This Agreement is not an admission of wrongdoing by Company.

Clause 4 - Release of Claims
Claimant releases Company from all claims related to wages through the Effective Date.

Clause 5 - Confidentiality
The Parties agree to keep the terms of this settlement confidential, except as required by law or as necessary to their attorneys or accountants.

Clause 6 - Non-Disparagement
Neither Party shall make disparaging statements about the other.

Clause 7 - Return of Property
Claimant shall return all Company property within five (5) days of the Effective Date.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Arizona.
