CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 1, 2026, between Fairwind Consulting Group ("Consultant") and Delta Health Systems ("Client").

Clause 1 - Scope of Services
Consultant shall conduct a ninety (90) day operational efficiency assessment and deliver a written report with recommendations.

Clause 2 - Fees
Client shall pay Consultant a flat project fee of $48,000, payable in three installments: $16,000 at signing, $16,000 at the 45-day midpoint, and $16,000 upon delivery of the final report.

Clause 3 - Expenses
Client shall reimburse Consultant for pre-approved travel expenses incurred in connection with the engagement.

Clause 4 - Independent Contractor
Consultant is an independent contractor and is not an employee or agent of Client.

Clause 5 - Confidentiality
Both parties shall protect the other's confidential information for five (5) years following termination of this Agreement.

Clause 6 - Non-Solicitation
During the engagement and for twelve (12) months afterward, Consultant shall not solicit Client's employees to leave their employment.

Clause 7 - Termination
Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice, paying Consultant for services rendered to date.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
