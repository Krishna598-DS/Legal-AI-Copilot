LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of February 15, 2026, between Summit Community Bank ("Lender") and Ridgeline Hardware LLC ("Borrower").

Clause 1 - Loan Amount
Lender shall provide Borrower a term loan in the principal amount of $150,000.

Clause 2 - Interest Rate
The loan bears a fixed interest rate of 7.25% per annum.

Clause 3 - Repayment
Borrower shall repay the loan in sixty (60) equal monthly installments of principal and interest, beginning thirty (30) days after the funding date.

Clause 4 - Prepayment
Borrower may prepay the loan in whole or in part at any time without penalty.

Clause 5 - Late Fee
If a payment is more than ten (10) days late, a late fee of 5% of the overdue payment applies.

Clause 6 - Collateral
The loan is secured by a lien on Borrower's business equipment.

Clause 7 - Events of Default
An event of default includes missed payments beyond the applicable cure period, insolvency of Borrower, or breach of covenants. Upon default, Lender may accelerate and demand the full outstanding balance.

Clause 8 - Financial Covenants
Borrower shall maintain a debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.25.

Clause 9 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.
