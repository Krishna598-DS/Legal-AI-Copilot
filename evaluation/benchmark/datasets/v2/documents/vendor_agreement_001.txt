VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Vendor Supply Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2026, between Brightline Office Supplies ("Vendor") and Falcon Logistics Inc. ("Buyer").

Clause 1 - Products and Pricing
Vendor shall supply the products listed in Schedule A at the prices listed, fixed for twelve (12) months from the effective date.

Clause 2 - Minimum Order
Buyer shall place a minimum monthly order of $2,000.

Clause 3 - Payment Terms
Payment is due net 30 days from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Clause 4 - Delivery
Vendor shall deliver orders within five (5) business days of order placement.

Clause 5 - Quality and Returns
Buyer may return defective products within fourteen (14) days of receipt for full credit.

Clause 6 - Term and Renewal
This Agreement has an initial term of one (1) year, automatically renewing for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides forty-five (45) days' notice of non-renewal.

Clause 7 - Exclusivity
This Agreement is non-exclusive. Buyer may purchase similar products from other vendors.

Clause 8 - Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
