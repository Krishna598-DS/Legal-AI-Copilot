EMPLOYMENT OFFER LETTER

Brightpath Learning Inc. ("Company") is pleased to offer Morgan Ellis ("Employee") the following position, subject to the terms below.

Employee is eligible for health insurance, a 401(k) plan with a 4% company match, and twenty (20) days of paid time off annually, per Company policy.

Employment with Company is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time, with or without cause or notice.

This offer is contingent upon successful completion of a background check and reference verification.

Clause 8 - Acceptance
This offer will expire if not signed and returned within seven (7) days.
