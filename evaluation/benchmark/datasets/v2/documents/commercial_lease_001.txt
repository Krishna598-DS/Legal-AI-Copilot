COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made as of February 1, 2026, between Meridian Retail Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Anchor Coffee Co. ("Tenant") for the premises located at 500 Market Street, Suite 4, Austin, TX ("Premises"), consisting of approximately 1,200 square feet of retail space.

Clause 1 - Term
This Lease is for a term of five (5) years, commencing April 1, 2026, and ending March 31, 2031.

Clause 2 - Base Rent
Tenant shall pay Landlord base rent of $6,500 per month, due on the 1st day of each month, with a 3% increase on each annual anniversary of the commencement date.

Clause 3 - Common Area Maintenance
Tenant shall pay its pro-rata share of Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges, estimated at $800 per month, to be reconciled annually against actual costs.

Clause 4 - Security Deposit
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $13,000 (equal to two months' base rent) prior to occupancy.

Clause 5 - Use of Premises
The Premises shall be used solely for the retail sale of coffee and related goods. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises for a different use without Landlord's prior written consent.

Clause 6 - Maintenance
Landlord is responsible for maintaining the structure, roof, and HVAC systems. Tenant is responsible for maintaining the interior of the Premises and all fixtures Tenant installs.

Clause 7 - Renewal Option
Tenant may renew this Lease for one additional five (5) year term by providing written notice at least 180 days before the expiration of the initial term, at then-current fair market rent.

Clause 8 - Default and Termination
If Tenant fails to pay rent and such failure continues for five (5) days after written notice, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon ten (10) days' additional notice.

Clause 9 - Insurance
Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

Clause 10 - Governing Law
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
